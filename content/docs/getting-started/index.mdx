Kumo is a native macOS app that routes traffic through the
[Mihomo](https://github.com/MetaCubeX/mihomo) proxy core. You bring your
own subscription; Kumo gives you a polished SwiftUI app, a friendly menu
bar item, and — if you want — a command-line tool and automation hooks
that match the GUI feature-for-feature.

If you have ever used a Mac proxy app and felt like it was either too
sparse or buried under a wall of settings, Kumo is built to land in the
middle. Daily controls — start, stop, switch nodes, toggle system proxy —
are one click away. The deeper knobs are there when you need them, and
they are not in your face when you do not.

## What you will need

- A Mac running **macOS 15 Sequoia or newer**, on Apple Silicon
  (M1 / M2 / M3 / M4).
- A proxy **subscription URL** from your provider, or a YAML / Clash
  config file you trust.

<Callout type="info">
You do **not** need a VPN provider, a developer account, or a
NetworkExtension entitlement. Kumo never asks for the **Allow VPN
Configuration** prompt.
</Callout>

## What Kumo is not

- Not a VPN service. You need a subscription from somewhere else to
  actually go anywhere.
- Not a NetworkExtension. Kumo does not request the **Allow VPN
  Configuration** prompt and does not install a system VPN profile.
- Not Windows or Linux software (today). Kumo is macOS-only and targets
  macOS 15 Sequoia or newer on Apple Silicon.
