This section tracks Kumo's staged future work. It is intentionally short and
high-level: the goal is to make it obvious what direction the project is
moving in, what the design constraints are, and where current capability sits
relative to a known reference implementation.

If you want to influence the roadmap, open an issue or discussion on
[KumoApp](https://github.com/ProjectKumo/KumoApp) — these documents capture
intent, not contracts.

`planned` or `deferred` are not commitments. Items marked `implemented` are
already in shipping builds; everything else is in motion.
