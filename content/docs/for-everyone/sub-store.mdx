If you only have one subscription, you can skip this page. The simple
flow on the [Add your subscription](/docs/for-everyone/first-connection)
page is plenty.

If you juggle **two or more** subscriptions — for example a fast one
and a backup, or one per region — Kumo bundles a small companion app
called **Sub-Store** that keeps them all in one place and lets you mix
and match.

## What Sub-Store is for

- Hold several subscription URLs in one place.
- Combine them into a single output (one merged proxy list).
- Apply small tweaks (rename groups, filter nodes by keyword) without
  editing YAML by hand.
- Hand the output to Kumo as a profile, the same way a regular
  subscription does.

It is not strictly required. Many users live happily with one
subscription and never open Sub-Store.

## Open Sub-Store

Click **Sub-Store** in the Kumo sidebar. The first time you open it,
Kumo prepares the bundled backend in the background and shows the
Sub-Store interface in a native panel.

You see two main tabs:

- **Subscriptions** — your raw subscription URLs.
- **Combined** — outputs that merge one or more subscriptions.

## Add a subscription to Sub-Store

1. Go to the **Subscriptions** tab.
2. Click **Add subscription**.
3. Paste the URL, give it a name, and pick the right **format**
   (almost always **Clash**).
4. Save.

Sub-Store fetches the subscription and shows the node count.

## Combine subscriptions

1. Go to the **Combined** tab.
2. Click **Add combination**.
3. Pick which subscriptions to include.
4. Optional: filter or rename nodes.
5. Save.

Each combination gets its own URL that looks like
`http://127.0.0.1:.../share/...`. That URL is what you paste into Kumo
as a profile.

## Feed Sub-Store output to Kumo

1. Copy the Sub-Store share URL for your combination.
2. In Kumo, go to **Profiles → +**.
3. Paste the URL.
4. Save.

Kumo now has a profile that always reflects the current Sub-Store
output. Refreshing the profile in Kumo refreshes the merged result.

<Callout type="info">
Sub-Store runs **locally** as long as Kumo is running. The share URL
only works on your own Mac — nothing is exposed to the wider internet.
</Callout>

## Want to use your own Sub-Store backend?

Some users prefer to host Sub-Store themselves (on a NAS, a VPS, or in
a container) and have multiple devices share it. Kumo supports custom
Sub-Store backends — see
[Sub-Store (advanced)](/docs/power-user/sub-store-advanced) in the
Power User track.
